Update: Java4Shell exploitation attempts begin as vendors issue advisories

By Kilwhiss News

·

19 November 2026, 11:36

Attackers have begun scanning for and trying to exploit Java4Shell, the serious OpenJDK flaw disclosed on 17 November, SecurityWeek reports.

The bug, CVE-2026-114938, is a memory corruption issue in how OpenJDK 17.0.6 and later handle Base64 data. It can only be exploited on x86-64 processors supporting a feature called AVX-512 VBMI; ARM systems are not affected. Oracle and the OpenJDK project issued patches on the day of disclosure.

By early on 19 November, GreyNoise had logged more than 150 attack-shaped requests from five IP addresses, though it had seen no successful intrusions. WatchTowr said its honeypots were being hit by reconnaissance traffic and blind exploitation attempts, and Cloudflare also reported activity.

Meanwhile Cisco, Elastic, Red Hat and Siemens have published advisories describing which of their products are affected.
