Cybersecurity

Product cybersecurity

We integrate cybersecurity into every stage of development to ensure reliable, up-to-date solutions that are ready for an increasingly connected environment. We are committed to providing secure products and building trust, always acting transparently and responsibly.

Our vulnerability management and disclosure process follows the main European and national regulations:

Cyber Resilience Act (CRA): we apply a formal vulnerability management process and comply with the EU’s notification requirements, including the use of the Single Reporting Platform (SRP) when necessary.

RED-DA: we align our risk management and incident reporting with Spanish regulations on the security of networks and information systems.

Secure by design

We apply a Secure Development Lifecycle (SDL) based on international standards such as IEC 62443 .

Expert management

Our PSIRT team coordinates the intake, analysis and resolution of vulnerabilities following rigorous procedures and CVSS criteria.

Responsible collaboration

We promote responsible disclosure and provide a Safe Harbor framework for ethical security testing.

Proactive communication

We proactively provide information on mitigations, updates and protection measures when necessary.
