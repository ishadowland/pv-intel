Osaki Electric ransomware attack halts smart meter production and shipments, with no restart date set | SecurTek Security

Osaki Electric, a Japanese maker of electricity smart meters, said in a November 18, 2026 incident notice that ransomware encrypted data on company servers. The company confirmed the encryption at about 10:35 a.m. on November 17 and disconnected its systems from external networks around noon that day. Osaki shut down production related systems at its Saitama R&D and Manufacturing Center and at related sites operated by Iwate Osaki Electric and Osaki Platech, suspending production and shipments of smart meters and other products, and a November 19 follow-up reported that the halt continued.

The company has not set a date for restarting production or shipments and says the financial impact remains unknown. It established an emergency response headquarters led by its president and chief operating officer, reported the incident to police and relevant authorities, and is investigating with external specialists whether data was leaked. Osaki did not name the ransomware strain and has not reported that deployed meters were compromised; a leak site claim by Qilin is not confirmed by the company and is not treated as attribution in this item. Prepared as a Cybersecurity item, not a Breaches add, because no personal records count has been disclosed and data leakage remains under investigation.

Update, November 25, 2026: In a third report dated November 25, 2026, Osaki confirmed that data was sent from its internal systems to an external cloud storage service; the content and scope of the data sent are still under investigation. The company has asked the provider to preserve, isolate and delete the data and says it will not meet the attackers' demands. Entry was through its VPN device at about 1:30 a.m. on November 15, most likely a guessed password on an account without multi-factor authentication, and group company Osaki Electric Systems was accessed through the same device the same day. Production equipment and deployed smart meters were not affected. Production at the Saitama works, Iwate Osaki Electric and Osaki Platec restarts in stages from November 27, shipments of existing orders resume from December 1, and new orders resume from December 7.
