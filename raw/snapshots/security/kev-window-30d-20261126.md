CISA includes vulnerabilities in KEV when its criteria for known exploitation are met. Check whether the listed products and affected versions exist in your environment, then consult the original advisory and CISA’s required action. A practical triage workflow
Match vendor and product names to your asset inventory, then verify affected versions against the original vendor advisory.
Record the asset reference, exposure and assigned owner. Prioritize using your environment’s risk and the vendor’s guidance.
Apply the listed required action or a verified vendor mitigation. Record evidence and verify the result before closing the task.
