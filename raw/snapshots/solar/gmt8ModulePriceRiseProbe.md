Photovoltaic module prices are rising, but stagnant orders remain a hindrance.
27/11/2026

This week, some leading component companies in the country have started to raise prices, and some distributors have already started to negotiate with new prices. Yesterday and today, most of the leading domestic companies have started to submit their price intentions internally, and they are generally planning to raise prices. According to SMM statistics, the price increase range for Topcon components in this round is 0.02-0.04 yuan/watt. However, it is worth mentioning that the downstream demand in December and the first quarter of 2026 is poor, and there is no increase in overseas orders. The European region has already started a new round of price concessions, but the actual order volume is still low. It is expected to be an important obstacle to the actual price increase of components.
